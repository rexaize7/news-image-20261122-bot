An illustrated briefing board that sums up **Sunday, 22 November 2026** in
one 1024 × 1024 image: seven numbered story cards, each with its own scene
picture drawn for the event and a full caption — COP31 ends with no
fossil-fuel deal (Antalya, thermometer and globe), a drone smashes a flat
block in Dymer, Ukraine (the lit, broken tower), a sea drone hits a Russian
warship at Benghazi (warship and drone), Iran hits a Bahrain site and tankers
are struck near Hormuz (burning tanker), the Cikande candy-factory fire in
Indonesia (the burning factory), the Democrats' biggest US House majority
since 1992 (the Capitol dome), and the World Court's Rohingya genocide
ruling (the court temple) — plus a yellow ticker with six more stories
(Mexico, markets, EU–Indonesia trade, BepiColombo, Nigeria, MotoGP) and, at
the bottom, a stylised world map pinning all seven stories where they
happened. `main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) copies the
pre-drawn `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
