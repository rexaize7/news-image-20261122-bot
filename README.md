# The World in One Day — Sunday, 22 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **“Sum up 22 November's world news in one image”**
(news-image-20261122).

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) copies the pre-drawn
front page `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, on every run.

## What the image shows

A newspaper front page dated **Sunday, 22 November 2026**:

- **Lead — Antalya, Türkiye:** COP31 ends with no deal on fossil fuels
- **Dymer, Ukraine:** drone smashes a flat block, 14 missing
- **Benghazi, Libya:** sea drone hits a Russian warship in port
- **Bahrain & Hormuz:** Iran hits a Bahrain site; tankers struck
- **Cikande, Indonesia:** candy-factory fire, 24 body bags recovered
- **Washington:** Democrats win their biggest House majority since 1992
- **The Hague:** World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide of the Rohingya
- Also on Sunday: Mexico cartel raid, the AI market sell-off, the EU–Indonesia
  trade deal, BepiColombo at Mercury, the Nigeria boat disaster, and
  Marc Márquez's 2026 MotoGP title

The image is 1024 × 1024 PNG, drawn programmatically with large, high-contrast
type so every headline and caption stays readable at thumbnail size.
